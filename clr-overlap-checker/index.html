The tool queries current Government of Alberta ArcGIS services for GOA screening layers and uses the ABMI province-wide Wetland Inventory for wetlands. Results are intended for spatial screening and do not replace applicable legislation, approval standards, current regulatory direction, site-specific studies or consultation requirements. ABMI wetland results are mapped inventory information and do not replace a site-specific wetland delineation.
